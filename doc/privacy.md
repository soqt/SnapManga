# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 17, 2026

LifeManga (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the LifeManga mobile application (the “App”).

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information
- We use **Sign in with Apple** to authenticate you. We receive a unique user identifier from Apple. Your email address and name are only stored if you choose to share them with us.
- We do **not** collect or store passwords.

### 1.2 Photos and User Content
- The photos you select for manga generation are processed on your device and temporarily sent to our servers (Supabase Edge Functions) to call OpenAI’s image-generation API.
- Generated images and original photos are stored **locally on your device** unless you explicitly share them.
- We do **not** use your photos to train AI models.

### 1.3 Purchase Information
- In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We receive a transaction identifier and credit balance through Apple’s receipt verification to credit your account.
- We do **not** collect or store payment card information.

### 1.4 Usage Data
- We collect minimal technical data (e.g., crash logs, anonymized usage statistics) through Apple’s services to improve app stability.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To provide the manga generation service.
- To manage your credits and purchases.
- To sync your project, item, and character metadata across devices via Supabase.
- To respond to support requests.

## 3. Third-Party Services

- **Apple** – Sign in with Apple and App Store in-app purchases.
- **Supabase** – Cloud authentication, database, and serverless functions.
- **OpenAI** – Image and story generation. Your photos are sent to OpenAI only when you initiate a generation.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

- Metadata (projects, items, characters) is stored in Supabase as long as your account is active.
- Local photos remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App.
- You can delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time in **Settings → Delete Account**. This action is irreversible.

## 5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 6. Your Choices

- You can revoke Sign in with Apple access in your Apple ID settings.
- You can delete the App to remove locally stored photos.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the effective date.
